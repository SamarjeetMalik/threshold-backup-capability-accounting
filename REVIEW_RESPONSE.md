## Claim audit

The strongest general results are relative mathematical characterizations, drawing on established monotone-derivation and linear-sharing ideas. The paper does not assert that the underlying mathematical foundations are novel. The independent-generation theorem adds a complete historical-view statement under its stated assumptions; it is not an active proactive-sharing protocol. The witness optimization may be exponential. TLC's 316,332 safe states are the sum of four runs, and unsafe runs are negative controls, not successful configurations. Regression tests do not certify physical cleanup.

The major revision is therefore a formal-analysis contribution with explicit component boundaries. External assessment of originality, completeness and venue suitability remains a research-review obligation.
